# Movement regression checks

```
node tools/tests/movement-checks.cjs              # all checks (about 6 minutes; `hsTouchCycle` alone runs about 2)
node tools/tests/movement-checks.cjs zones stops  # only some
```

Run from the repo root. The script starts its own local server, opens `movement.html` in headless Chromium (Playwright; install with `npm i -D playwright` if it's missing) and checks the real controller, animator and posture code against limits taken from the approved movement. Run it after any change to movement, animation clips, the rig or tuning, and before testing on the phone.

| Check | What it guards |
|---|---|
| `zones` | Real touches on an iPhone-landscape screen. A light push is SNEAK (1.0–1.3 m/s), a medium push STEALTH WALK (1.85–2.05), a full push JOG (3.1–3.3), and crouched SLOW / FAST CROUCH. A thumb wobbling ±2 px at a zone edge never flips the gait |
| `controls` | No RUN button. CROUCH toggles on and off. The stick and the camera drag work at the same time |
| `plantedFeet` | Planted-foot slip while walking at every gait (≤ 0.4 m/s) |
| `stops` | Releasing the stick: she stops within 1 s and settles into idle, with the planted foot still. The jog stop's last stride drags the foot a little (≈ 0.7 m/s, known), so its limit is 0.8 |
| `fingers` | Fingers don't drift or spin over 10 s (≤ 10°) |
| `jointPops` | 60 s of random stick input, stance changes, turns, stops and starts. No single-frame joint pops while crouched, measured relative to the body. Standing fast reversals are counted but not failed |
| `footsteps` | Footstep noise events: one per foot landing, alternating feet, in step with the gait cycle; level 1 for sneak and slow crouch, 2 for stealth walk and fast crouch, 3 for jog; none while standing or crouching still |
| `surfaces` | The surface strips: the map reports concrete, metal and grass where they are (concrete elsewhere). Footsteps on each strip carry that surface, the gait level stays 1 / 2 / 3, movement speed is identical, and the reach is grass < concrete < metal at every gait |
| `footstepAudio` | Each surface's footstep event plays its own sound. Rendered offline at a stealth-walk step, metal is loudest (> 1.2× concrete) with a ringing tail, concrete is dry, grass is quietest (< concrete / 1.2) and brightest |
| `visionZones` | The guard's nested zones: at 6 m in clear view, each sample's zone matches the angle he measured (30° / 70° / 110° total) on both sides, the angle matches where she was placed (±2.5°), fill rates fall from fastest to normal to slower, and none outside. Widening a zone or doubling its rate in the settings takes effect |
| `visionFactors` | Fill rate falls with distance and is zero past the profile's range; crouching is slower than standing; moving faster than still, jogging faster than walking; at 1.8 m in clear view she's recognised in under 0.4 s |
| `visionCover` | Simple Cover: the 2 m crate hides her standing (meter stays 0); behind the 0.9 m crate she's seen standing but hidden crouched; with long range and the guard facing her, a 2.8 m wall blocks him completely (never seen through), while the same spot in front of it is seen |
| `visionDecay` | After losing sight the meter holds (within 2% at 0.5 s), then falls gradually (below the hold, above 5% at 2 s) to zero by 5 s, never rising while unseen |
| `hearingRange` | Synthetic footstep events through the noise system: heard inside the event's own reach (surface included) × sensitivity, ignored outside (concrete L2 at 5.5 m heard, 6.5 m ignored; metal L2 at 9 m heard where concrete isn't; grass L2 ignored at 5 m, heard at 3 m); Keen and Poor profiles shift it; the guard's own sounds are ignored |
| `hearingSurfaces` | Real footsteps on each strip at sneak, stealth walk and jog: heard 0.3 m inside each step's reach and ignored 0.3 m outside; metal > concrete > grass at every speed; sneak < stealth walk < jog on every surface; standing still on each strip makes no footsteps and nothing is heard |
| `hearingTurn` | A sound 90° to his right: he starts turning after the reaction delay (not before), turns at no more than his turn rate (no snap), faces the sound's origin within 3° while the player stands silent elsewhere; hearing alone leaves the vision meter at 0; after the turn the player at that spot is in his fastest zone (the cone turned with him). Since Step 5 he must still face the sound after the old 3-second look timer, and return to his post facing only once his suspicion has faded (within 30 s) |
| `hearingJitter` | 10 s of real footsteps jogging circles behind him: look redirects (now counted by his awareness) stay spaced by the retarget gap, the turn rate changes smoothly (< 0.75 rad/s per frame), no rapid reversals, no joint pops on the guard |
| `aiSuspicion` | A faint grass step barely registers (stays NORMAL); one sneak step makes him SUSPICIOUS but not investigate, and he's calm and facing his post again later; stealth-walk steps repeated around one spot build suspicion step by step until he investigates, with no joint pops or rapid turn reversals |
| `aiInvestigate` | Two jog steps behind him: approach → search → return → NORMAL, in that order; his target stays the sound's origin while the player moves away unseen; he comes to rest 0.3–1.8 m from it, searches over 60° of directions standing still, and is back at his post and NORMAL within 60 s, never seeing her |
| `aiNavigation` | A spot behind the tall crate (straight line blocked): he reaches it by a detour without ever overlapping an obstacle, and returns. A spot outside the perimeter wall: "unreachable", he doesn't move, never pushes into a wall, searches from where he stands and settles back |
| `aiVisionAlign` | While walking a detour: his hips stay within 30° of his vision facing, his travel within 15° when walking straight, and spot checks put a point dead ahead in his fastest zone and one square to his side outside the cone |
| `aiConfirmed` | In plain view he's confirmed (meter 100%); she then vanishes silently out of view and 8 sounds come from his right over 20 s: the confirmation is kept and he never drops back to NORMAL / SUSPICIOUS / INVESTIGATING (Step 6: ALERT, then EVASION); his first search spot is the last place he actually saw her; that position never follows her; the sounds are remembered separately; the confirmation outlasts the vision meter fading |
| `alertTrack` | Full recognition → ALERT; while she walks across his view he turns to track her (facing within 30° of her, never leaving ALERT); standing still 10 s she stays ALERT, no search timer starts, he faces her within 8° and doesn't head back to his post |
| `evasionStart` | She slips behind the tall crate: EVASION starts only after the grace period, and his first search spot is the last place he truly saw her. She then moves away unheard: his search stays within the evidence area (hide radius + prediction + spacing of the last sighting) and never homes in on where she really is (> 8 m); last-seen never moves |
| `evasionHearing` | During EVASION: a faint nearby sneak step doesn't redirect him; metal jog steps behind a 2.8 m wall do (reason names the sound), toward an estimate that's off the true spot but within its spread (a close strong sound gets a tighter spread); 14 s of footsteps can't push the search past its cap (40 s), redirects stay spaced, no joint pops or rapid turn reversals |
| `evasionSearch` | 30 s without evidence: at least 3 different spots (none closer than the spacing to another: no pacing between two places), no obstacle overlap, HEIGHTENED SECURITY at 30 s, which opens standing still in idle (a pause to look around); a 10 s setting ends at 10 s; another random seed plans a different search order |
| `hsCycle` | Step 7 full cycle with her hidden far away: EVASION → HEIGHTENED → RETURNING → NORMAL in that order; Heightened Security lasts its setting (60 s ± 0.5); at least 2 checkpoint walks and 3 look-around pauses; he walks slower than in the search (< 0.8×), every checkpoint within his leash (12 m) of the post; he stands down ≤ 0.4 m from his post and ≤ 4° from its facing, turning no faster than his turn rate; still NORMAL, standing and idle 5 s later; no per-frame jump (< 6 cm, < 4.6°), no obstacle overlap, he never saw her; afterwards a sound is heard and she's seen in front of him |
| `hsInterrupt` | A sighting during Heightened Security and during the return → ALERT as soon as his meter reaches the re-alert level (the stand-down cancelled and noted), then a fresh search; 40 s of faint steps never extend it; a meaningful sound sends him to his estimate (not the exact spot) and adds ≤ 5 s; a stream of strong sounds: extensions ≥ 8 s apart, total capped at 90 s, then he returns; a strong sound during the walk back interrupts it, he checks, then returns and stands down at his post; in plain view for 100 s he's never NORMAL |
| `evasionReacquire` | Seeing her again mid-search returns him to ALERT ("saw her again"); losing her again starts search #2 with a fresh 30 s |
| `spawnSafe` | The spawn: every one of the 9 vision body points is beyond his range or outside his cone; standing still there 20 s with him watching and listening gives a meter of 0, NORMAL only and no footsteps; ≥ 1.5 m clear of every obstacle, ≥ 3 m from him, inside the yard; the camera starts behind her along her facing, looking into the yard |
| `resetClean` | Real touches on an iPhone-landscape screen: after he's alerted and searching, she's crouched and moving and he's heard a sound, RESET puts her at the spawn (position and facing exact), standing, not crouched, camera behind her; him at his post facing it, NORMAL, suspicion 0, no memory/search/investigation/sound left, meter 0. Only the step she was taking may finish (≤ 2 settle steps in 2 s, unheard by him), then nothing for 3 s; she's in idle and he's still |
| `hsTouchCycle` | Real touches, frame loop running (about 2 minutes): from the spawn she stealth-walks into view until he's alerted, jogs back out of range and north along the west wall, and hides. He runs the whole cycle ALERT → EVASION → HEIGHTENED (60 s ± 1) → RETURNING → NORMAL, stands down ≤ 0.4 m and ≤ 4° from his post, never sees her while she's hidden, is never NORMAL while seeing her, and never jumps (< 15 cm per frame) |
| `reactFirst` | Step 8: three jog steps behind a calm guard (enough to investigate at once): no head or body movement before `reactDelay`, then exactly one reaction and one vocal request at `reactDelay` (± 0.05 s); the head (≤ its 2.6 rad/s rate) and body (≤ turn rate) start turning after it; his first investigation step comes no earlier than `reactDelay + assessTime`; no guard joint pops |
| `reactRepeat` | Ten sneak steps 0.6 s apart: one incident, one vocal reaction, suspicion rising on every step below its 100% cap, the reaction time set once (never restarted), one head turn (not restarted), no rapid body-turn reversals; then ten stealth-walk steps while he walks: his walk is never interrupted and there are no more reactions |
| `reactSight` | A sound starts his pause, then she's 1.6 m in front of him in plain view: ALERT on exactly the same frame as with no sound, and the vocal reaction cancelled. Then EVASION → HEIGHTENED → RETURNING → NORMAL still completes with sounds throughout, and no new reaction |
| `reactRecalm` | One sneak step: one reaction. Another just after he settles (inside the calm time): none. After a genuine calm (incident over): a new incident with its own reaction |
| `reactTouch` | Real iPhone touches: she crosses the concrete / metal / grass strips behind him at sneak, stealth walk and jog. Each run: ≥ 3 steps heard, at most one incident and one vocal reaction. No floating ?/! text, sprites or icons anywhere (scene and page); his bones are not shared with the player's |
| `reactCaption` | Step 8.1, the TEMPORARY "Huh" caption (iPhone-landscape page, frame loop running). Ten footsteps in one incident: shown exactly once with one vocal request, never restarted (its opacity rises, holds, falls once). On screen 1.2–1.9 s of game time, about 1 s at full opacity. Reads "Huh" at ≤ 14 px (< 24 px tall), within 12 px of the point above his head. Hidden behind the tall crate and from 30 m. A new incident that turns ALERT dismisses it at once |
| `groundContact` | In the crouch idle both feet touch the ground (lowest skinned vertex ≤ 3 cm) |
| `clearance` | Limbs stay out of the body: hand to shin ≥ 15 cm in the crouch idle (≥ 12 cm during the crouch-walk arm swing), thigh to spine ≥ 15 cm |

## Fixed issues

- **Knee jump on a sharp turn out of the crouch idle** (found by `jointPops`): starting to move reset the stride to the passing pose even while the turn-in-place steps were already showing the walk clip, so the legs jumped (≈19° at the back knee). Now the stride only resets when that jump would be invisible (`startPhaseSnap` in `animator.js`).
